Gustav Klimt

A founding member and first president of the Vienna Secession, Gustav Klimt's work is characterized by an elegant synthesis of Symbolism and Art Nouveau. His 'Golden Phase' produced some of the most recognizable, opulent portraits of the early 20th century, elevating the decorative arts to profound psychological depths.
